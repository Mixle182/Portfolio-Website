About Mixle Philippines

Mixle Philippines is a digital content creation page that shares visual work centered on album cover art, nature photography, and quote-based graphic design. The page's output blends photography and typography into pieces meant to be shared and enjoyed on social media, from moody album artwork to calming nature shots paired with reflective quotes.

What I Contribute

My contributions to Mixle Philippines span photography, visual design, and content creation — including mini photography series like the Flower Photography set featured in the banner above, along with quote-driven graphics and album cover concepts.

Album Covers

Original cover art concepts designed for music releases and playlists.

Nature Photography

Mini photography series capturing flowers, landscapes, and natural detail.

Quote Designs

Typography-driven graphics pairing reflective quotes with visual design.

My Role & Purpose

Working on Mixle Philippines lets me exercise the creative side of my skill set — visual design, photography, and content creation — outside of formal development work. It reflects an interest in blending imagery and words into something that resonates with an audience, complementing the more technical, structured work found elsewhere in this portfolio.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To create visual and typographic content — album covers, nature photography, and quote-based designs — that resonates with people and brings a calming, reflective presence to their social feeds.

Vision

To grow into a recognizable creative page known for thoughtful, aesthetic content that blends photography and words into something people genuinely want to pause and enjoy.
