About the Brand

The UMak Student Multimedia Organization brand centers on communication, creativity, and campus representation. It reflects a strong student media identity that values clarity, professionalism, and community engagement across journalism, creative work, and digital storytelling.

Brand Direction

The visual language for USMO is designed to feel confident, approachable, and credible, while still being dynamic enough for youth-led digital media. The system supports a range of content formats, from printed publication layouts to social media graphics and campaign visuals.
