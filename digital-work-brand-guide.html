University of Makati Brand Guide

UMak · Official visual identity and design system

About the UMak Brand Guide

The University of Makati brand guide defines the official identity of the institution, from the seal and logo system to the color palette, typography, and proper usage rules. It presents a clean and professional visual direction that reflects the university’s academic standards, institutional values, and public identity.

What the guide covers

This guide organizes the university’s visual identity into clear components: the official seal, approved logo variations, allowable color system, typography recommendations, and standards for proper logo application. The result is a documentation system that keeps all communications aligned, consistent, and recognizable.

Official Seal and Logo

The University of Makati seal is the official visual representation of the institution. Its approved full-color, gray, and gold monochrome versions should remain clear and recognizable.

Meaning Behind the Seal

The torch represents guidance and service. Its handle reflects the university’s institutional history, while the rays, gear, and triangle represent Makati’s communities, business roots, and commitment to technology.

Color System

The primary palette combines UMak blue, UMak yellow, and silver lake blue. Department colors provide a consistent way to distinguish academic units while preserving the larger university identity.

Typography and Layout

Marcellus is recommended for institutional names, headings, and sub-headings. Metropolis supports body copy and secondary information with a clean, readable structure.

Correct Logo Usage

The seal must not be stretched, recolored, redrawn, replaced with outdated versions, or combined with unauthorized elements. These rules protect the credibility of the university brand.

Identity Goals

Mission

To build a recognizable and professional institutional identity that strengthens public trust, academic credibility, and visual consistency across all official materials.

Vision

To provide a clear visual framework that helps the university project a modern, polished, and cohesive brand across print, web, and communication design.
