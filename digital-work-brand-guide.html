University of Makati

The University of Makati is a city-supported higher education institution committed to accessible, relevant, and quality education. Its identity reflects academic excellence, public service, innovation, and a strong connection to the Makati community.

About the UMak Brand Guide

The UMak Brand Guide defines how the university presents itself across official communication. It brings together the seal, logo applications, colors, typography, and layout standards into one consistent visual system that keeps every design recognizable and professional.

What the Guide Covers

The guide explains the meaning of the official seal, approved logo variations, institutional colors, recommended typefaces, department color identities, and correct logo usage. These standards help protect the university’s history, values, and public identity.

Official Seal and Logo

The University of Makati seal is the official visual representation of the institution. Its approved full-color, gray, and gold monochrome versions should remain clear and recognizable.

Meaning Behind the Seal

The torch represents guidance and service. Its handle reflects the university’s institutional history, while the rays, gear, and triangle represent Makati’s communities, business roots, and commitment to technology.

Color System

The primary palette combines UMak blue, UMak yellow, and silver lake blue. Department colors provide a consistent way to distinguish academic units while preserving the larger university identity.

Typography and Layout

Marcellus is recommended for institutional names, headings, and sub-headings. Metropolis supports body copy and secondary information with a clean, readable structure.

Correct Logo Usage

The seal must not be stretched, recolored, redrawn, replaced with outdated versions, or combined with unauthorized elements. These rules protect the credibility of the university brand.

Identity Goals

The UMak Brand Guide establishes a consistent visual identity for the University of Makati. It brings together the official seal, approved logo applications, institutional colors, typography, and layout standards so every university communication remains recognizable, professional, and connected to the institution’s history and values.
