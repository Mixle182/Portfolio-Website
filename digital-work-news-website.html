About USMO

The UMak Student Multimedia Organization (USMO) is the University of Makati's officially recognized campus journalism organization and student publication. Its mission centers on promoting inclusivity and diversity across campus media, encouraging critical thinking, and developing the next generation of student journalists and media practitioners. USMO is organized into four departments — Broadcast Media, Print Media, Social Media, and Creative Media — each contributing to the organization's overall publishing output.

What USMO Publishes

The website organizes USMO's journalism into distinct content categories, each with its own purpose on campus.

News

Timely coverage of university announcements, events, and student affairs.

Features

In-depth stories on campus life, student achievements, and organizations.

Opinions

Editorial and commentary pieces on issues affecting the student body.

Literary

Creative writing, essays, and literary works from student contributors.

Publications

Official magazines and print releases produced by the organization.

Science

Science-focused features and reporting for the student community.

About This Project

Co-developed the official website for USMO, giving the organization a single, reliable platform to publish across all of its content categories instead of relying solely on social media. Built on WordPress and hosted through Hostinger, the site features a responsive UI and a streamlined content management system that lets writers, editors, and department heads publish and manage stories independently.

WordPress Hostinger CMS

My Role & Purpose

As part of USMO's Social Media Department (January 2025 — June 2025), this project let me apply my Computer Science background in web development to a real organizational need: giving a student-run publication a professional, sustainable home for its journalism. It reflects the practical, user-focused approach I bring to development work — building something a non-technical editorial team could rely on and manage long after my involvement.
